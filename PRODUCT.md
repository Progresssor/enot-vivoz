# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS in one page (index.html + assets), deployed on Vercel. Chosen because the owner is a first-time builder and the site is a single landing page.

## Users

- Private clients doing an apartment or house renovation who need debris gone without hassle.
- Foremen and crews who order containers repeatedly and want speed and a predictable price.
- Construction companies with larger objects who need a contract, cashless payment and documents.
- Dacha and plot owners clearing land or demolishing a shed.

Most visitors arrive on a phone, often on site.

## Product Purpose

Construction waste removal in Moscow and Moscow Oblast: a container (8, 20 or 27 m³) is delivered to the object, picked up full, and the waste is taken away for disposal. Success: the visitor calls or messages (WhatsApp / Max) to get a price.

## Positioning

The price is named before departure and fixed: it does not change on site. Delivery time is agreed in advance.

## Operating Context

Order flow: call or message → choose container and agree price → container delivered at agreed time → full container taken away. Payment in cash or cashless; works under contract.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Containers: 8, 20, 27 m³.
- Prices are NOT shown on the site by owner's decision; copy says "call or write, we name the price right away".
- Phone: 8 967 155-03-03 (also WhatsApp and Max). Telegram is not used.
- Company name is undecided; placeholder "ЕНОТ ВЫВОЗ" until the owner chooses.

## Brand Commitments

- Colors: black and bright green (as on the branded container), with small signal-yellow accents from container graphics.
- Mascot: raccoon in green cap, green sunglasses and hi-vis vest showing thumbs up (`enot.webp`).
- Container slogan: «Чистота начинается с нас!»; container side text: «Профессионально. Надёжно. Оперативно».

## Evidence on Hand

- Mascot image: `enot.webp`.
- Photo of a real branded container: `container.webp`.
- Owner has more photos of containers/trucks and client reviews, and works under contract, but has not supplied them yet. Do not invent reviews, counts, years or client names.

## Product Principles

1. One action: get the price by phone or messenger, reachable from every screen.
2. Fixed price is the promise; say it plainly and repeat it.
3. Speak to both a private renovator and a foreman: concrete volumes and jobs, no fluff.
4. The raccoon carries the brand's warmth; the service stays serious and reliable.
